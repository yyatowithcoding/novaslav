A Constructed Language

Novôslav

A from-scratch vocabulary wrapped in a clean, consistent grammar. Strict word order, articles that tack onto the end of the noun, and verbs that only change for tense, written in plain Latin letters with a few accented ones for their own sounds.

Framework

How it's put together

Vocabulary

A from-scratch word list with a soft, diphthong-heavy sound: the consonants ď, ť, ľ, ň, plus the extra vowels ä ö ô.

Grammar

Strict Subject-Verb-Object order, no case system, definite articles tacked onto the end of the noun (-en / -et), and verbs that only conjugate for tense, never for person or number.
